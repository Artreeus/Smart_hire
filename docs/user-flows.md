# User Flows

## Job seeker

1. Create an account and verify the email token.
2. Complete profile details and upload a CV.
3. Review extracted skills, experience, and suggested roles.
4. Browse or filter jobs and inspect explainable match details.
5. Save promising roles or submit an application.
6. Track employer status updates and interview details.
7. Report a listing when necessary.

## Company

1. Create a company account.
2. Complete the company profile and upload a logo.
3. Submit official information and evidence for verification.
4. Create a job and review its safety analysis.
5. Publish or submit the job for human review.
6. Review incoming applicants and AI match evidence.
7. Shortlist, interview, select, or reject applicants.

## Administrator

1. Sign in through the admin account.
2. Monitor live platform statistics.
3. Review companies and supporting evidence.
4. Review reported or safety-flagged jobs.
5. Resolve reports, moderate listings, and manage accounts.
